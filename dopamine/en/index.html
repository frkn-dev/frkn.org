How to install on macOS

  1. Open the downloaded Dopamine-arm64-4.8.14.45.pkg (Apple Silicon) or Dopamine-intel-4.8.14.45.pkg (Intel).
  2. If macOS shows "Apple could not verify…" — click "Done", then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Dopamine → confirm with your password → "Open".
    Or run once in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/Dopamine-*.pkg — the PKG will open right away.
  3. Follow the installer — at the end the system will ask for the administrator password (this installs the system VPN service, that's normal).
  4. The app launches automatically after installation — all set.
